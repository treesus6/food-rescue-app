# In-App Legal Notice (Good Samaritan Food Donation Act)

**Version: RCW_69.80.031_v1**

Not legal advice. Have a Washington attorney or a food-recovery nonprofit's legal contact review this exact wording before shipping it to real users.

## Full version (shown on a donor's first-ever post)

You're protected by law. Washington's Good Samaritan Food Donation Act (RCW 69.80.031) protects you from liability when you donate food in good faith, even if it's past a "best by" date, as long as it's still wholesome. A person is not subject to civil or criminal liability arising from the nature, age, packaging, or condition of apparently wholesome food donated in good faith to a nonprofit for ultimate distribution to needy individuals, with the only exception being gross negligence or intentional misconduct. Just use the same judgment you'd use feeding your own family, and you're covered.

## Short version (receipt/sticker footer)

Donated under RCW 69.80.031 (WA Good Samaritan Food Donation Act) — good-faith donations of wholesome food are legally protected.

## The important caveat

The Act only protects food that already meets quality and safety standards — visibly contaminated, putrid, or decomposing food is NOT covered. The app's category/freshness-window system exists to keep every donation inside that "good faith" line, not just as a UX nicety.
